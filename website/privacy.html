Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Summary

ZecBooks does not upload your viewing keys, ledger rows, memos, or proof packs to SaneApps servers. Books stay on your Mac. Network use is limited to Zcash lightwalletd sync and optional Sparkle update checks from this site.

Data Collection

The Mac app does not collect:

  • Personal information or account profiles
  • Viewing keys, seeds, spend keys, or passphrases
  • Ledger contents, memos, tags, or proof packs
  • Crash reports sold to advertisers

Local Storage

ZecBooks stores vault and preference data locally on your Mac. Proof packs you export live where you save them.

Network Access

The app may contact:

  • Zcash lightwalletd: view-only sync for notes visible to an imported viewing key
  • Sparkle updates: checks https://zecbooks.app/appcast.xml when enabled

Website

This website may use cookie-free Cloudflare Web Analytics. That applies to the website only, not the Mac app.

Source

Source is available under PolyForm Shield at github.com/sane-apps/SaneBooks.

Contact

Questions: hi@saneapps.com or open a GitHub issue.
