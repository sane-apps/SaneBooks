Proof pack vs CSV and PDF

CSV and PDF are convenient. The proof pack is the controlled share format. Know which job you are doing.

By SaneApps · August 4, 2026

The short answer: Use the encrypted proof pack when someone else must review scoped books with integrity and expiry. Treat CSV/PDF as plaintext exports that do not expire and do not carry the same guarantees.

Quick comparison

FormatBest forRisk note
Proof packAccountant / reviewer handoffScoped, encrypted, expiring — preferred share path
CSVYour own spreadsheetsPlaintext, non-expiring
PDF summaryPrintable overviewPlaintext, non-expiring

Pick with intent

  1. External review → proof pack

    Someone else needs evidence without your vault. Pack wins.

  2. Your Excel model → CSV

    You are the only consumer and you accept plaintext on disk.

  3. Printed packet → PDF

    Use for summaries you are willing to leave in a folder forever.

Common mistakes

  • Emailing CSV and calling it “the same as a pack.”
  • Assuming PDF redacts memos you already exported in full.
  • Reusing an old plaintext export after classifications changed.
